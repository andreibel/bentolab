# Global Timeline

The global timeline shows issues from all boards in the organization on a single Gantt chart. Use it to spot conflicts across teams and track organization-wide epics and milestones.

## Who can do this

All organization members can view the global timeline.

## Steps

1. Click **Timeline** in the global navigation section of the left sidebar (not the board-specific Timeline — this one is at `/timeline`).
2. Issues from all boards you have access to appear as bars, color-coded by board.
3. Use the board filter at the top to show or hide specific boards.
4. Interact with bars the same way as the board-level timeline — drag to resize dates, click to open issue detail.

## Troubleshooting

- **Board not appearing** — You must be a member of the board for its issues to appear. See [Board Permissions](./boards/permissions.md).
- **Issues from a board missing** — Issues must have both a start date and a due date to appear on the timeline.
