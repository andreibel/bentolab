# Calendar

The calendar view displays issues on a monthly calendar based on their due date. Use it to get a day-by-day sense of upcoming deadlines and workload distribution.

## Who can do this

All organization members.

## Steps

1. Click **Calendar** in the left sidebar (or navigate to `/calendar`).
2. The current month is shown with issues placed on their due dates.
3. Navigate between months using the previous and next controls in the calendar toolbar.
4. Click an issue chip on the calendar to open the issue detail view.
5. Use the board filter to show issues from a specific board or all boards.

::: info Beta limitation
The calendar view is read-only in the current beta release. Dragging issues between dates to change due dates is not yet supported.
:::

## Troubleshooting

- **Issues not appearing** — Issues must have a due date to appear on the calendar. Set the due date from the issue detail view.
- **Too many issues on one date** — The calendar shows a condensed chip view when many issues share a date. Click the date cell to see a full list.
