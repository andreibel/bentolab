# Switch Organization

If you belong to more than one organization, you can switch between them using the organization switcher. Each organization has its own boards, members, and settings — switching changes the entire context of the app.

## Who can do this

Any member who belongs to more than one organization.

## Steps

1. Click the organization name in the top-left corner of the sidebar. A popover appears listing all organizations you belong to.
2. Click the organization you want to switch to.
3. The app reloads in the context of the selected organization.

## Troubleshooting

- **Organization not appearing in the switcher** — You may not have accepted the invitation yet. Check your email for an invitation link. Accepted invitations take effect immediately.
- **"Access denied" after switching** — Your role in the new organization may restrict access to certain pages. Contact your organization admin.
