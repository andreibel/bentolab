# Profile

Your profile contains your public-facing name, avatar, and locale setting. Other members see your name and avatar on issue cards, comments, and assignee pickers.

## Who can do this

Any authenticated member (yourself only — you cannot edit other members' profiles).

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Settings → Profile** (or `/settings/profile`).
2. Update your full name and any other editable fields.
3. Click "Save" to apply changes.

## Avatar upload

1. Click the avatar image on the profile page.
2. Select an image file from your device. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP.
3. Crop the image in the crop tool that appears, then confirm.
4. The new avatar is saved and displayed across the app immediately.

## Locale

Your locale setting controls the language of the entire Bento UI and the text direction (LTR for English, RTL for Hebrew).

1. On the profile page, find the locale field.
2. Select your preferred language from the dropdown.
3. Click "Save". The page reloads in the selected language and direction.

See [Preferences](../settings/preferences.md) for the full locale and theme settings page.

## Troubleshooting

- **Changes not saving** — Confirm you clicked "Save" after making changes. The page does not auto-save.
- **Avatar not updating** — Check the file format and size. Very large images may be rejected. <!-- TODO: confirm with maintainer — avatar size limit -->
- **Wrong language after saving** — Clear your browser cache and reload if the language does not switch immediately.
