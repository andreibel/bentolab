# Time Tracking Analytics

The time-tracking analytics page aggregates time log entries across all issues, showing how hours are distributed by team member, board, and time period.

## Who can do this

All organization members.

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Analytics → Time Tracking** (at `/analytics/time-tracking`).
2. Use the date range filter to select the period you want to analyze.
3. Use the board filter to focus on a specific board or view all boards.
4. The chart shows total hours logged per assignee. The table below the chart lists individual time log entries.

## Troubleshooting

- **No data appearing** — Time logs must exist. Team members log time from individual issues. See [Time Tracking](../issues/time-tracking.md).
- **My time logs not appearing** — Confirm you are a member of the board whose issues you logged time on.
