# My Issues

My Issues shows all issues assigned to you, across every board in the organization. Use it as your personal work queue and to keep track of what needs attention.

## Who can do this

Any organization member.

## Steps

1. Click **My Issues** in the left sidebar (or navigate to `/my-issues`).
2. The page lists all issues assigned to you across all boards, sorted by last updated.
3. Click any issue to open its detail view.

## Filters

Use the filter controls at the top of the My Issues page to narrow the list:

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| Status | Open, In Progress, Done, All |
| Priority | Urgent, High, Medium, Low, None |
| Board | Specific board or all boards |
| Sprint | Active sprint, specific sprint, or all |
| Due date | Overdue, due this week, due this month |

Multiple filters can be active simultaneously. The filter state is preserved when you navigate away and return.

## Troubleshooting

- **Issue not appearing** — Confirm the issue is assigned to your account. Open the issue and check the assignee field.
- **Issue from another board missing** — Confirm you are a member of that board. See [Board Permissions](../boards/permissions.md).
