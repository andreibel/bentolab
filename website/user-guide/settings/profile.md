# Profile Settings

The profile settings page is where you update your public-facing name, avatar, and contact information.

## Who can do this

Any authenticated member (your own profile only).

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Settings → Profile** (at `/settings/profile`).
2. Update your name and any other available fields.
3. Click "Save" to apply changes.

See the full [Profile](../accounts/profile.md) guide for details on avatar upload and locale settings.

- **Changes not persisting** — Confirm you clicked "Save". The profile page does not auto-save.
